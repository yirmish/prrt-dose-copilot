PRRT Dose Copilot

A dose predictor that knows when to measure · built on TabPFN-3.5

Before 177Lu-DOTATATE therapy every patient has a 68Ga-DOTATATE PET; the absorbed dose is measured only after therapy. On our cohort the PET predicts that dose only partly — so the tool returns a calibrated dose distribution and, at each clinical threshold, says decisive or measure.

Research prototype · not a medical device · the app runs on a synthetic cohort; real-cohort results are aggregates.

What we measured on the real cohort

One centre, cycle-1 treatments. 5×5 cross-validation grouped by patient, identical folds for every model, TabPFN-3.5 offline, untuned, native quantiles. Comparators nested-tuned.

Why published numbers look better

Same model, same data: R² 0.81 when lesions of one patient sit on both sides of the split, 0.41 when the split is by patient. The kidney control (about one row per patient) shows almost no gap (0.21 vs 0.15).

What we do not claim

  • A confirmed accuracy advantage: the lesion-level result is a discovery plus a registered robustness check; confirmation is registered and needs about 100 new patients.
  • An accuracy win with PET features alone.
  • That predicted dose is precise enough to plan therapy — a 95% tumour interval spans about 10×.
  • That the app's lesion intervals are calibrated — with 17 PET features they are too narrow (95% covers 0.89).
